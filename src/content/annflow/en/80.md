title: "2) A layer on Space: tap = space, hold = F18"
---

This is my basic layer and one of the most successful in terms of ergonomics.

The logic is simple: **space stays space**, and **holding space** turns into a "mode service key" (in my case the virtual **F18**). Hammerspoon then listens for F18 and turns on the command layer.

Why Space: it's in the centre of the keyboard and always under the thumb. It's the most natural "mode button", because the thumb is almost always free and easy to hold down without getting in the way of typing.

The key advantage of this approach is that it doesn't break text input. I type as usual: a quick tap on space always gives a space. And when I need control, I just **hold Space** and get a temporary command layer that lives exactly as long as the hold is active.

The most important nuance here is **hold timing**. I **retuned** this layer and **rewrote the JSON** several times until I found a comfortable to\_if\_held\_down\_threshold\_milliseconds value: so that the mode doesn't turn on by accident while typing, but turns on instantly when needed.
