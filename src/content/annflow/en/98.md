---
title: "Two mood knobs: style and energy"
---

In [Without Streaming](https://t.me/without_streaming) I made the controls **like a mixing desk**:
one knob shifts the broadcast Jazz ⇄ Hip-hop, the other changes the energy Drifting ⇄ Driving.

The basis is deliberately simple and transparent: I tag tracks in Telegram (#J / #H / #JxH and #drift / #drive / #dd). An indexer collects this into playlist.json, and on a /playlist?mix=&energy= request the server rebuilds the track order. #JxH and #dd are tracks I myself consider mixed in style (Jazz×Hip-hop) and tracks of mixed energy (between drift and drive).

Then the code does a simple thing: it **turns the slider positions into coefficients** of "how much attention to give each side" and calculates the track's final score. The higher the score, the higher the track rises in the /playlist output.

```
jazz_bias   = 1.0 - hiphop_bias

drive_bias  = energy / 100.0
drift_bias  = 1.0 - drive_bias

score = jazz_bias * w_jazz + hiphop_bias * w_hiphop \
      + drift_bias * w_drift + drive_bias * w_drive
```

- mix and energy turn into \*\_bias coefficients: this is "how much influence" to give each side of the scale.
- w\_jazz / w\_hiphop / w\_drift / w\_drive are track weights derived from tags during indexing.
- score is the final rating: the higher it is, the sooner the track will be on air at the current settings.

Then an important engineering part comes in: to keep the broadcast from looping within a narrow category, the server first serves the "matching" tracks (and bridges), then mixes in the rest.

Why it's not perfect yet: tags are subjective, two axes create many combinations, and "musically right" doesn't always match "highest score".

**Auto modes (Auto Flow / Auto Energy)** move the knobs smoothly and don't break the current track; the effect shows on the next one.
By the way, **auto mode can be turned on right from the slider**: press the "dot" (the slider) and it starts moving on its own.
