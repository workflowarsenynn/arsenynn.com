title: "Radio from Telegram in one click"
---

I have a Telegram channel, [Without Streaming](https://t.me/without_streaming), where I record my personal music library, as well as short video cuts from my projects [@arsenynn](https://t.me/arsenynn) and [workflow@arsenynn](https://t.me/ww_arsenynn). For me it's not just a selection but **an imprint of taste and time**.

It all started pragmatically: come to a gathering, a party or get into a car, and instead of connecting to a speaker or player, just open your channel on the phone of whoever is already connected and turn on music in one step.

**Music and GIFs** work here as a compact record of a state: together they preserve the mood of a moment faster and more precisely than any explanation.

**Telegram as a CMS.** A new post in the channel automatically becomes part of the broadcast, **with no separate admin panel or media library**.

**Visuals as a "TV channel".** One visual repeats three times, then switches to the next; the starting set is shuffled every time.

**Audio is streamed on the fly from Telegram without being saved to disk.** This is a conscious choice: minimum infrastructure and minimum "traces".

**The technical projection** is simple: FastAPI + Telethon → JSON playlist → chunked stream
