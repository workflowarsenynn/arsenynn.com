---
title: "What this project is really made of"
---

From the outside it looks simple: a chat, a bot, replies. But in fact this project is built as **a system of several layers**.

The first layer is the **Telegram interface**: messages, commands, media, entry points and the interaction with people itself.

The second layer is the **SQLite database**. It stores messages, users, profiles, evidence, media and service state. Otherwise the bot would remain a creature with a memory one chat window long.

The third layer is **context logic**. The bot relies not only on the last message but on the history of communication, the person's profile, previous reactions, internal rules and accumulated material.

The fourth layer is **profiling**. The project gradually gathers a structure of observations about users: what they feel close to, how they speak, which motives recur. Thanks to this, replies become less random and more personal.

The fifth layer is **work with media material**. The bot doesn't only talk, it also takes part in recirculating content: it returns old files to the stream, links them to the conversation and doesn't let material vanish after publication.

And finally there are **background processes**: updating profiles, rotating content, service states, separate cycles that keep the whole system alive.

So it's more accurate to say this: I made not just a Telegram bot but **an environment** that connects **memory, profile, context, a media library and the logic** of bringing material back to life.
